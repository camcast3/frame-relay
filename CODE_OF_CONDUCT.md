# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Examples of positive behavior include:

- being respectful and constructive
- assuming good intent while discussing technical disagreements
- accepting correction and taking responsibility
- protecting other people's privacy and security

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention,
doxxing, publishing private logs or credentials, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces
where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behavior confidentially through GitHub private reporting and prefix the report
title with `Code of Conduct`:

<https://github.com/camcast3/frame-relay/security/advisories/new>

Reports will be reviewed confidentially where possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
